About this calculator

What it calculates

The chance of getting a mutation (Albino or Piebald) from your eggs. Both have the same chance. Which one you get depends on the parents' skins, which this tool doesn't track.

How it's calculated

  1. Chance per egg
    P = 0.005 × (1 + female pearl + male pearl)
    The base chance is 0.5%. Each pearl is written as a decimal (5% = 0.05). A parent without a pearl adds 0.
  2. Total eggs
    nests × eggs per nest (the number depends on the species).
  3. Odds you'd have one by now
    (1 − (1 − P)eggs) × 100
    The chance that at least one of your eggs was a mutation.
  4. Average
    1 ÷ P eggs. With no pearls that is 1 in 200 eggs.

Pearls scale the base chance, they don't add percentage points: a 5% pearl makes 0.5% become 0.525%.

Good to know!!

You can reach the average number of eggs and still not get a mutation. That's how probability works: every egg is an independent roll, there is no pity system, and nothing here is a guarantee. The odds are a statistic, not a promise.

Where the info comes from

The formula and the pearl behavior come from information shared by the Day of Dragons community. It was not taken from the game's code, so it may be wrong or change if the game is updated.
